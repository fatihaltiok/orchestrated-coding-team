# Literature search of 27 Sept 2026: what "opened and read" means

Source: the search report of 27 Sept 2026 (private repository of the tools, report "Related work on the
coding team"), opening line of its section on citable sources. Dated excerpt, translated from German:

> All sources were opened during the search, mostly the abstract page. Where only an abstract was read,
> numbers should be checked in the full text before citing them.

So "about 100 sources were opened and read" in the paper means: the 103 sources listed in
`literature-sources.csv` were each opened, and at least their abstract page was read. The report also
lists sources it did not open and therefore does not cite; they are not counted.
